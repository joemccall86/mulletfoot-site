# **Runner’s World: Couch to 5K Training Plan**

This 10-week plan is designed for beginners to safely transition from walking to running a full 5K (3.1 miles).

## **Key Training Principles**

### **1\. The Warm-up and Cool-down**

* **Warm-up:** Start every session with 5 minutes of brisk walking to prepare your muscles and joints.  
* **Cool-down:** End every session with 5 minutes of gentle walking followed by stretching while your muscles are still warm.

### **2\. Pacing**

The running intervals should be done at a "conversational pace." If you are gasping for breath and cannot speak a full sentence, you are running too fast.

### **3\. Strength and Mobility**

On your "Rest" days (specifically Mondays and Fridays), consider adding light strength training or yoga. Focus on:

* **Glutes and Hips:** To stabilize your stride.  
* **Core:** To maintain good posture as you tire.  
* **Calves:** To prevent common injuries like shin splints.

### **4\. Listening to Your Body**

* If you feel sharp pain (not just muscle soreness), stop and rest.  
* If a specific week feels too difficult, do not hesitate to repeat that week before moving on to the next.  
* Consistency is more important than speed.

### **5\. Gear**

Ensure you have proper running shoes. Most "lifestyle" sneakers do not provide the support or cushioning needed for repetitive impact on pavement.
